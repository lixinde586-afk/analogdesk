[limits]
This analysis has concrete limits. The engine's own validation states: "Analog retrieval hits its coverage target out of sample and its width tracks each instrument's own volatility, but it is NOT sharper than a same-name unconditional band at matched coverage, and its raw PIT distribution fails a uniformity test. It is a stress-testing and provenance instrument, not an alpha source." The directional hit rate is 50.1%. At matched coverage, the analog conformal interval is 10.2% wide, while the unconditional same-name interval is 9.48% wide, meaning the retrieval adds -7.6% sharpness versus just using the name's own history.
