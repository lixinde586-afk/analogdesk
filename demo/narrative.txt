[limits]
This analysis has concrete limits. The engine's own validation states: "Analog retrieval hits its coverage target out of sample and its width tracks each instrument's own volatility, but it is NOT sharper than a same-name unconditional band at matched coverage, and its raw PIT distribution fails a uniformity test. It is a stress-testing and provenance instrument, not an alpha source." The conformal interval achieves 80.8% out-of-sample coverage with a width of 9.95%, but the matched coverage sharpness versus the same-name unconditional benchmark is -7.6%, meaning the naive benchmark is tighter at the same coverage.
