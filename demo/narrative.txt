The three scenarios most relevant to this specific idea are the 2020-02/03 liquidity crash, because it produces the largest negative delta versus baseline (-6.26%) and the highest 10% drawdown breach rate (58.3%) across 48 analogs, directly testing what happens when the elevated 45.77% realised vol meets a systemic bid-ask blowout; the 2018-Q4 tightening scare, because the current state already features a quiet benchmark vol (9.16%) alongside high name-specific vol, and this scenario isolates rate-driven multiple compression, dragging the median to -3.02% across 50 analogs; and the 2024-08-05 carry unwind, because it specifically targets crowded positioning and gap risk, yielding a -2.11% median across 36 analogs with a 16.7% probability of a 10% drawdown breach. A reader should distrust the heavy concentration of recent dates in the retrieval: 22 of the 50 analogs come from 2026 and 16 from 2025, meaning the distribution is overwhelmingly shaped by the last two years rather than a broad historical base. The 2023-03 regional bank stress scenario contradicts the downside assumption of the idea, producing a positive median of 1.51%, but it reflects sector rotation rather than a broad de-rating.
