[limits]
Validation, out of sample on calibration 2019-01-01..2022-12-31, test 2023-01-01..2026-09-18, 2698 out-of-sample queries over 71 instruments: the conformal analog interval covered 80.8% of realised outcomes against a 80% target (cluster-robust standard error 1.95 pp), at a mean width of 10.4%. It is not the sharpest interval available. At matched coverage the same-name unconditional band is 9.48% wide against 10.2% for the analog band, a difference of -7.6%. Per-symbol coverage dispersion is 8.5 pp for the analog band, 7.2 pp for the same-name band and 14.8 pp for the pooled band. Probability calibration fails a uniformity test: PIT chi-square 208.6 against a 5% critical value of 16.92. Directional hit rate of the analog median is 50.1%, i.e. no better than a coin toss, which is the expected result for daily-feature equity prediction and is stated rather than hidden. Engine's own verdict: Analog retrieval hits its coverage target out of sample and its width tracks each instrument's own volatility, but it is NOT sharper than a same-name unconditional band at matched coverage, and its raw PIT distribution fails a uniformity test.
